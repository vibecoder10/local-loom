## Ryan's tutorial kit (16:9) acceptance checks

Run after changing `templates/tutorial-16x9/` or `scripts/*-tutorial.mjs`.

1. **Scaffold refuses the wrong take.** `scaffold-tutorial.mjs` on a `camera` or `shorts-screen` take exits with a plain-language message and creates nothing. Observable: non-zero exit, no project folder.
2. **Plan errors say what to change.** A scene gap, an overlay past the end, or a zoom region outside 0..1 fails `build-tutorial.mjs` naming the scene/overlay index. Observable: message text, no `index.html` written.
3. **Lint is clean.** `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.41 lint`: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
4. **Snapshots at every scene boundary and overlay.** House style holds: canvas `#D9DCDF`, hard offset shadows, orange used selectively, no soft shadows; the clean screen has no bubble or captions baked in; no stray marks before an overlay starts.
5. **Callouts land.** A callout's ring sits on its (x, y) in the screen clip, also while a zoom is active (check with a static feature).
6. **Render.** 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, duration equals `manifest.duration.finished` within 0.1 s, zero decode errors, audio not silent.
7. **Captions follow speech.** Captions appear only while words are spoken; the orange word matches the audio at three spot checks; presenter-scene captions are centred with no plate.
8. **Registered.** The kit has a library entry and `library/index.html` rebuilds with it.
